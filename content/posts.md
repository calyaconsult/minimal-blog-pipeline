A static blog can be **simpler** than a database-backed CMS.

When your posts live in plain text, the whole publishing system becomes easier to back up, version, and move.

## Why it helps

- Your source of truth stays readable.
- You can rebuild the whole site at any time.
- RSS stays under your control.
